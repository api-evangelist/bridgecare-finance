# BridgeCare

> BridgeCare (getbridgecare.com) is an integrated, modular SaaS platform for the early care and education (ECE) system, used by state agencies, cities and counties, child care resource and referral organizations (CCR&Rs), nonprofits, and school districts to run Pre-K administration, family navigation, coordinated eligibility and enrollment, licensing, workforce development, quality management (QRIS), grants management, subsidy administration, and program assurance. Backed by Techstars.
